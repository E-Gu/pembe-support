Pembe Support

Let’s get you learning.

For help with Pembe, email support_apple@fromzero.guru.

No sound

Check your device volume and the selected audio output. If headphones are connected, sound may be playing through them. Check playback speed in Pembe Settings.

Speaking exercises

Allow microphone and speech recognition access for Pembe in iOS Settings. In Pembe Settings, under Microphone, tap the phone icon for the built-in microphone or the headphones icon for a compatible headset. Speech recognition may require an internet connection.

Learning progress and updates

Update the existing app without deleting it. Guest progress stays on your device. If you sign in, use the same sign-in method and Pembe account on each device. Transfer existing guest progress only after checking the account destination. For Pembe Pro, open Settings → Pembe Pro → Restore Purchases; deleting an account does not cancel its Apple subscription.

Deleting the app may remove your local learning data. Do not reinstall it as a first troubleshooting step.

Tell us what happened

Include your Pembe version, iOS version, device model and a short description of the issue. A screenshot can help, but remove any unrelated personal information first. Never send passwords.

Privacy questions

Read the Pembe Privacy Policy or contact support_apple@fromzero.guru.
