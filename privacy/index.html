Pembe Privacy Policy

How Pembe handles learning data, accounts, speech and subscriptions. Processing depends on the features you use.

Pembe is provided and operated by Sergii Paranko. For data and privacy questions, contact support_apple@fromzero.guru.

With and without an account

You can start learning without an account: guest progress, preferences and custom cards stay on your device. Removing the app may remove that data. Device backups are controlled by your Apple settings.

Versions supporting synchronization let you sign in with Apple or a one-time email code when that method is available. We process an account identifier and your email, including Apple's private relay address when selected. Use the same sign-in method on each device; matching email addresses do not automatically combine accounts. Sign-in tokens stay in secure device storage.

Learning synchronization

After sign-in, the account synchronizes card progress and review dates, learning history and daily totals, mistakes, custom decks and card text, card reports and shared preferences. Existing guest progress is uploaded only after you confirm its transfer. Conflicts keep both versions for you to choose. Microphone preferences and an unfinished exercise remain on your device.

Pembe's separate PocketBase server is hosted by Hetzner in Hillsboro, United States. One-time email sign-in codes are sent from support@fromzero.guru through Google Workspace. Account, installation and device identifiers, IP addresses and technical request information support authentication, synchronization and abuse prevention. This data is not used for targeted advertising.

Pembe Pro subscriptions

Where subscriptions are available in your version, Apple processes payments and renewals. RevenueCat, Inc. verifies and restores purchases and determines access to paid features. RevenueCat receives a pseudonymous identifier (a random anonymous identifier, or one linked to your Pembe account after sign-in), purchase and transaction details, products, subscription dates and status, and technical information required by its SDK. Purchase information also supports sales analytics in the RevenueCat dashboard.

Our code does not send RevenueCat your email, learning progress, card text or voice recordings, or enable advertising attribution or advertising identifier collection. Pembe does not receive payment card details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Apple's privacy information.

You can purchase or restore without an account: RevenueCat then uses a random anonymous identifier, and Pro is available on this device. After sign-in, the purchase is linked to your Pembe account; to use Pro on another device, sign in with the same account or restore purchases with the same Apple ID. The current price, currency and period appear before purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled in Apple settings. Expiry restricts paid features without deleting learning progress. Subscription terms use the standard Apple EULA.

Microphone and speech recognition

Speaking exercises request permission to use the microphone and Apple's speech recognition service. Recognition may run on your device when supported, or use Apple's servers. Your speech may therefore be processed by Apple. Internet access may be required. Pembe does not upload voice recordings to its synchronization server or to RevenueCat.

You can withdraw microphone or speech recognition permission in iOS Settings. Exercises requiring those permissions will then be unavailable.

Reference recordings and diagnostic journal

The Turkish words and examples supplied with Pembe are bundled recordings created during development. Pembe does not send your microphone recordings to ElevenLabs to create this reference audio.

Pembe keeps a short listening diagnostic journal in memory only while the app is running. It may contain recognition results and technical events. It is not saved to storage, is cleared when the app closes and is not sent to the developer.

Retention and backups

Account data remains until account deletion. Cloud migration and resolved-conflict snapshots remain for 30 days after resolution; unresolved versions remain until resolved. Local pre-upgrade safety backups remain until the corresponding profile is deleted. Resolved local recovery versions are pruned after 30 days during the next synchronization; cleanup is deferred while offline.

The server is configured for daily backups retaining the seven latest automatic copies. Backup copies are stored in Cloudflare R2 (Cloudflare, Inc.). Backups support recovery, and deleted active data may remain in them until scheduled rotation. PocketBase technical logs retain five days; web-server logs are configured for 14 daily rotations.

Logout and account deletion

Logout removes sign-in tokens while retaining unsynced changes in a sealed profile for your next sign-in. Another account does not receive those changes.

Delete Account is available in Settings → Account after reauthentication and confirmation. It removes active cloud data and the current account's profile on that device. Other offline devices clear the corresponding profile on their next server contact. Associated Apple tokens are revoked for Apple sign-in. The original separate guest profile remains.

Account deletion also requests erasure of the linked RevenueCat profile. If that service is unavailable, the process remains pending until a successful retry; RevenueCat processes accepted requests asynchronously. Deleting your Pembe account does not cancel an Apple subscription. Cancel it separately in Apple subscription settings. Payment records retained by Apple are governed by Apple's rules.

For questions about access, correction or deletion of your data, email support_apple@fromzero.guru.

Support requests

If you email support, we receive the email address and information you send and use them to respond. Send only what is needed to explain the problem. You can request deletion of support information by contacting the same address.

Apple services and this website

App Store and TestFlight distribution, operating-system permissions, speech recognition and device analytics you choose to share are also governed by Apple's privacy information.

These support pages do not contain a registration form or an analytics script added by the developer. The hosting service may process technical connection information needed to deliver the website.

Policy changes

We update this page when Pembe's data handling changes. The update date appears at the top.
